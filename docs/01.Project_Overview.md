# Organic Powder — Project Overview

## 1. Project Name

Organic Powder

## 2. Project Type

Direct-to-consumer e-commerce platform for organic
fruit, vegetable and other food powders.

## 3. Project Purpose

Organic Powder is a full-stack e-commerce platform
designed to make natural food products easier to
discover, understand and purchase through a modern
digital shopping experience.

The platform also provides an internal operations
system for managing products, inventory, orders,
shipping, marketing, finance, customer support,
content, AI and analytics.

## 4. Business Vision

Make natural food products easier to discover,
understand and purchase through a trustworthy
digital experience.

## 5. Core Platform Areas

### Customer Platform

Customers can:

- Browse products
- Search products
- Filter products
- View product details
- Select product variants
- Add products to cart
- Apply coupons
- Checkout
- Make payments
- Track orders
- Manage wishlist
- Manage addresses
- Write reviews
- Receive product recommendations
- Interact with the AI assistant

### Operations Platform

The internal Admin Portal manages:

- Products
- Inventory
- Orders
- Shipping
- Marketing
- Finance
- Customers
- Customer support
- Content and SEO
- AI features
- Analytics
- Procurement
- Notifications
- Audit logs
- System settings

The platform uses role-based access control so that
employees receive only the permissions required for
their responsibilities.

### AI Platform

AI is used to assist customers and internal teams
with:

- Product discovery
- Product recommendations
- Semantic search
- Product description drafts
- SEO metadata drafts
- Recipe suggestions
- AI-assisted product information

AI-generated information must be reviewed where
appropriate before being published.

AI is not the source of truth for:

- Product price
- Product stock
- Product availability
- Payment status
- Order status

These values come from the application's
authoritative data sources.

## 14. Engineering Principles

The project will follow these principles:

- Keep code readable
- Use meaningful names
- Keep functions focused
- Separate responsibilities
- Avoid unnecessary complexity
- Handle errors properly
- Validate external input
- Protect sensitive operations
- Document important decisions
- Write comments only where they provide useful context
- Test important success and failure cases

## 15. Project Scope

The initial system will be developed as a modular
MERN application.

The architecture should remain simple enough to
develop and maintain while allowing the platform
to evolve as the business grows.

The initial project is not intended to be:

- A multi-vendor marketplace
- A medical platform
- A full ERP
- A microservices-based distributed system

Future capabilities can be introduced when the
business requirements justify them.

## 16. Long-Term Vision

The long-term vision is to evolve Organic Powder
from a simple online powder store into an
intelligent natural-food commerce platform.

Potential future capabilities include:

- Subscriptions
- Product bundles
- Loyalty programs
- Referral programs
- Gift cards
- Multiple warehouses

- ERP integrations
